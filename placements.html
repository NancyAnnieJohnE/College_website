Training & Placement Cell

The Training and Placement Cell of Christ College of Engineering and Technology plays a vital role in shaping the career of students. It bridges the gap between academia and industry by organizing recruitment drives, internships, skill development programs, mock interviews, and industry interactions.

Campus Recruitment

Leading IT and core companies visit the campus every year for placement drives offering excellent career opportunities.

Internship Programs

Students are encouraged to take part in internships with reputed organizations to gain real-world experience.

Soft Skills Training

Regular aptitude training, communication skill development, and personality development sessions are conducted.

Industry Interaction

Workshops, seminars, and guest lectures are organized to expose students to current industry trends.
